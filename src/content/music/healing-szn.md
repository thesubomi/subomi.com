February to August 2026 was healing. September onward is thriving. This playlist covers both. It opens loud, with Till I Collapse, Asake’s Forgiveness and Common’s Blue Sky, then settles into melodic house from Marsh, Nox Vahn and Ben Böhmer for the long, quiet stretches. Somewhere in the middle it turns into Afro house, and by the end it is all Kanye, Drake, Meek Mill and Eminem. Play it front to back if you are rebuilding something, yourself included.
