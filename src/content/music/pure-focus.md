This is the playlist I resort to when I need undivided focus and to get things done. Long melodic house and techno with almost no lyrics, so nothing pulls at your attention: Thylacine, NTO, Romain Garcia, Township Rebellion, and two from Worakls, whose music speaks directly to my soul. Four and a half hours, which is about one good working day if you take a lunch break.
