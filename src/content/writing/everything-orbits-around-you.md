## Everything runs through you

In the early days of a company, almost everything passes through the founder. Decisions. Approvals. Direction. It makes sense. Speed matters, and most of the context lives in your head.

## The waiting begins

As the team grows, something subtle starts happening. People begin waiting. Waiting for your sign-off. Waiting for your opinion. Waiting for you to enter the room. Without realising it, you become the center of gravity.

## It doesn’t feel like a problem

From your perspective, everything feels normal. You’re being helpful. You’re staying involved. You’re keeping quality high. But from the team’s side, progress now depends on your availability. That’s where execution quietly starts to slow.

## Scale requires a system

Real scale doesn’t come from making more decisions. It comes from designing a system where good decisions happen without you. Clear ownership. Simple frameworks. Trust to move without perfect information.

## Reducing leadership gravity

Great leadership isn’t about being in every conversation. It’s about removing yourself from as many conversations as possible. While keeping the quality of outcomes high.

## The real bottleneck

If everything still needs to come back to you, you don’t have a leadership role. You have a very busy operational job. And that’s often the hidden reason execution slows as companies grow.
