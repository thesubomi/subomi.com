## Podcasts

There’s no better way to start my day. Listening to Omar break down business principles into clear, bite-sized ideas is my definition of a perfect morning. Diary of a CEO is my go-to for learning from people who have built companies at the level I’m working toward. And of course, the list wouldn’t be complete without a selfish shoutout to my own podcast. Go listen.

## Books

It’s only two months into 2026 and I’m already on my fifth book. I set a goal of 12 books this year, but it’s starting to feel more like a Q1 goal. I get lost in books easily. Especially non-fiction. Lately, I’ve been experimenting with fiction. Honestly, it’s not really my scene yet. But let me finish the one I’m reading now and I’ll share an update in a few days.

## Croissants

If you have some time on your hands, go to YouTube and search how croissants are made. I’ve loved croissants since the very first time I laid eyes on them. But watching how much love, care, and PATIENCE goes into making them took that love to another level. My appreciation for these beauties skyrocketed. Eat a croissant today.

## Music

J. Cole is the definition of my come up. If you know, you know.

“The dollar and a dream” philosophy sits close to my heart. Starting out, there wasn’t much. We simply made do with what we had. Watching J. Cole chase his dreams and actually achieve them made me believe I’m capable of the same.

Worakls makes this list because his music speaks directly to my soul. If you see this post, shoutout to you, bro.

## Movies

Call me old school, but my all-time favorite movie is The Sound of Music. So many lessons in that story, but I’ll leave you with one: “Good things come to those who wait.” If you know, you know.

The cinema is my go-to for new releases. Recently enjoyed watching Crime 101 featuring Chris Hemsworth and Halle Berry.
