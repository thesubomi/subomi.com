Personal brands are not built by accident. They are built through consistent ideas shared over time. The right tools make this easier. Here are the ones I use the most.

## ChatGPT

**Thinking partner.** Asides refining my writing, GPT supports with brainstorming, pressure tests, design feedback and exploring ideas. One idea can quickly turn into multiple content directions.

## Notes

**Idea capture system.** Ideas rarely arrive on schedule. They show up during conversations, while reading, during walks. Notes is where I capture them before they disappear.

## Figma

**Visual storytelling.** Attention is visual first. Before people read your ideas, they see your design. Carousels. Visual hierarchy. Typography. Design helps ideas travel further.

## Socials

**Where I share my thinking.** Ideas only build a brand when people see them. These are my main channels: LinkedIn, Instagram and Substack. This is where conversations happen. Follow me @subomidotcom on all of them.

## CapCut

**Video editing.** Video builds trust faster than text. CapCut keeps the editing simple. Quick cuts. Captions. Clean exports. From concept to output in minutes. Shoutout to team CapCut.

## Riverside + Spotify for Creators

Riverside handles recording. Podcasts. Video conversations. Teleprompter for intros. Spotify for Creators handles distribution. One conversation can become a podcast episode, video clips and multiple posts.

---

You don’t need a box of tools. Just a few that help you capture ideas, create content, share your thinking, and show up consistently. That last one is the most important part of it all.
